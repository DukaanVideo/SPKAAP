# 🚀 Sapna Pura Karenge (SPK App)

Welcome to the official repository of **Sapna Pura Karenge (SPK App)** — an AI-powered gaming ad generator designed for creating stunning promotional videos for games, Telegram, WhatsApp, and Instagram.

## ✨ Key Features:
- **Splash & Login Screen:** Simple mobile number and password login (No OTP hassle).
- **AI Video Studio:** 
  - Hero / Character Name input.
  - Plus (+) button to upload custom photos from the gallery (or let the AI generate a smart avatar).
  - Custom script, voice selection (Male/Female AI voice), and action styles.
- **Secure Download & Monetization:** Pay-per-download system (₹5 via UPI integration like Google Pay / PhonePe) to unlock the final video.
- **Ad History & Earnings:** Track total generated ads and earnings in real-time.
- **Strict Safety Policies:** Built-in content filters and strict legal warnings against misuse under Cyber Crime law.
